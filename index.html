How it works

Frequency first

Cards come from the most common words you don't know yet, so your study time goes where it pays off most.

Real forms, one word

"es", "fue" and "sido" are all forms of ser (to be). You see the forms people actually use, and your progress counts towards the dictionary word.

A known list that keeps up

Get a word right 9 times in your last 10 and it joins your known list. Miss it 3 times in a row and it comes back for practice.

Ten-minute sessions

Short sessions fit into your day. Each one ends with a review of what you knew and what to practise next.

Two languages

The 5,000 most common words in Spanish and in French, each in over 13,000 forms. Progress is kept separately for each language.

Private and offline

No account and no sign-up. Every word is built into the app and your progress stays on your phone. Privacy policy
